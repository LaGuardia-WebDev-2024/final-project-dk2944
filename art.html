Where I get my talent from

This is my mom, Deshira. She's the reason I do what I do and continues to inspire me every day. My mom has performed all over the world, from Lincoln Center and Carnegie hall to Turkey, Croatia, Belgrade, Romania, Italy, and so much more. She continues to be the prima donna of her Opera house in North Macedonia where she travels almost 3 times a year to perform.

This is my dad, Arian. He started his music career in new york in the early 90s. He has worked with big musicians like Nile Rogders who has written songs for artists like Madonna, Diana Ross, etc (they were seriously best friends). Later on, he created his own band called "Arian and the funky family" where his producer was grammy award winner Hubert Eaves. The picture to the left is his album cover and his songs are amazing, take a listen!
