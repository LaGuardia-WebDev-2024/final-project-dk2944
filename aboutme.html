About Me

I am Dea Krliu, and I'm a passionate singer from New York. Music has been a huge part of my life for as long as I can remember. I've been performing since I was young-on stage, different events, and tv. I love sharing my voice and connecting with people through music. No matter what genre I'm singing, I always put my heart into every performance.

Over the years, I've had the opportunity to perform for some of the most amazing events. One was at Cipriani for an event honoring a worldwide famous photagrapher. This event meant so much to me because of the happiness it gave him. It also gave me the chance to sing in front of many celebrities like Martha Stewart, famous models, singers, and actors/actresses.
