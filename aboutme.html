About Me

My name is Dea Krliu, and I'm a passionate singer from New York. Music has been a huge part of my life for as long as I can remember. I've been performing since I was young-on stage, different events, and tv. I love sharing my voice and connecting with people through music. No matter what genre I'm singing, I always put my heart into every performance.

Over the years, I've had the opportunity to perform for some of the most amazing events. One was at Cipriani for an event honoring a worldwide famous photagrapher.
